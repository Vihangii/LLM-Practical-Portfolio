## My Contribution

My main tasks were the LLM research and the mock-up design.

For the research, I worked on understanding the spending problems that university students in Sri Lanka face. I went through the problems and product ideas from the LLM and checked them against our own experience as students, for example how most of our daily spending is cash and how allowance payments can come late. This helped us decide which problems were the most important for our product to solve.

For the mock-up, I described each screen and used an AI design tool to draft the five main screens of SpendSense: Home, Add Expense, Ask AI, Insights and Challenges. I then reviewed and edited the screens so they matched our product idea and the needs of our persona, such as showing one "safe to spend today" amount on the home screen and voice logging in English, Sinhala or Tamil.

Imadi wrote and ran the LLM prompts and created the presentation slides. We discussed the LLM's answers together and made the final decisions on the product as a group.
